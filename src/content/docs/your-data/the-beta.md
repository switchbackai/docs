Switchbak is in beta. During the beta, these terms apply:

- The service runs in an environment that is not stable. It can stop or change without notice.
- Switchbak does not guarantee the durability of your data. Do not use the warehouse as the only copy of a session.
- At the end of the beta, Switchbak deletes the data of your organization from the warehouse. The backups that hold it expire 30 days later. The other copies that erase does not reach stay, as [What erase removes, and what stays](DATA-NOTICE.md#what-erase-removes-and-what-stays) names.
